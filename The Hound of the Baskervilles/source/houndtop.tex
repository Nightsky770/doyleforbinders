My dear Robinson,\\

It was to your account of a West-Country legend that this tale owes its inception. For this and for your help in the details all thanks.

\begin{flushright}
Yours most truly,\\
\textsc{A. Conan Doyle.}

\textit{The Hound of the Baskervilles,} Arthur Conan Doyle's (1859--1930) third Sherlock Holmes novel, was serialised in \textit{The Strand Magazine} between August 1901 and April 1902 with illustrations by Sidney Paget (1860--1908). It was published as a standalone novel later in 1902 by George Newnes Ltd., with cover art by Alfred Garth Jones (1872--1955). Grateful acknowledgement is made to the Internet Archive, which in 2009 digitised original copies of the relevant issues belonging to the University of Michigan.
