The Hugo Baskerville story in chapter 2 uses old-timey ligatures and the short 's'. *I* like setting in-universe text in a fun font, and Sherlock does mention that short 's'es are used in the manuscript he's looking at, but I understand that it's not everyone's thing. I've placed the original non-ligatured version of Hugo Baskerville's story in the "chapters" folder, as "hugostory.txt", in case you want to slice out the ligatured version and paste the original back in.  
Fonts used are:

* EB Garamond https://www.1001freefonts.com/eb-garamond.font
* Essays 1743 https://www.thibault.org/fonts/essays
** Important! You must use v2.0 or above or you will not get the emdash.
* Zallman Caps https://www.1001fonts.com/zallman-caps-font.html
